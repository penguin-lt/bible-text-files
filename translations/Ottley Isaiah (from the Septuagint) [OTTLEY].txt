[Ottley Isaiah (from the Septuagint)] [1904] [OTTLEY]
[Isaiah] [1]:[1] The vision which Isaiah the son of Amoz saw, which he saw concerning Judah and Jerusalem, in the reign of Uzziah, Jotham, Ahaz, and Hezekiah, who reigned over Judah.
[Isaiah] [1]:[2] Hear, O heaven, and give ear, O earth; for the Lord hath spoken, I have begotten sons and upraised them, but they have set me at nought.
[Isaiah] [1]:[3] The ox knoweth his owner, and the ass his master's crib: but Israel knoweth me not, and the people understandeth me not.
[Isaiah] [1]:[4] Ah, sinful nation, a people full of sins, an evil seed, lawless sons: ye have forsaken the Lord, and angered the Holy One of Israel.
[Isaiah] [1]:[5] Why should ye be yet smitten, (for) transgressing further? every head (turns) to weariness, and every heart to grief.
[Isaiah] [1]:[6] From the feet to the head, neither wound, nor stripe, nor inflamed hurt: there is no means to apply a balm, or oil, or bandages.
[Isaiah] [1]:[7] Your land is desolate, your cities are burnt with fire; your country, strangers devour it before your face, and it has been desolated, ruined by stranger peoples.
[Isaiah] [1]:[8] The daughter of Zion shall be left like a booth in a vineyard, and like a watching-hut in a cucumber garden, like a city besieged.
[Isaiah] [1]:[9] And unless the Lord of Hosts had left us a seed there, we should have become as Sodom, and we should have been made like as Gomorrah.
[Isaiah] [1]:[10] Hear the word of the Lord, ye rulers of Sodom; attend ye to the law of God, O people of Gomorrah.
[Isaiah] [1]:[11] What is the abundance of your sacrifices to me? saith the Lord: I am full of burnt offerings of rams, and the fat of lambs, and the blood of bulls and of goats I desire not,
[Isaiah] [1]:[12] Not even if ye come to appear before me. For who hath demanded this of your hands? ‘Tread my court
[Isaiah] [1]:[13] Ye shall not, any more; if ye bring me fine flour, it is vain; incense, it is an abomination to me; your new moons, and the sabbaths, and a great day, I cannot bear: fasting and idleness,
[Isaiah] [1]:[14] And your new moons, and your feasts, my soul hateth; ye are become to me a surfeit! no longer will I let your sins be.
[Isaiah] [1]:[15] When ye stretch out your hands toward me, I will turn away mine eyes from you; and if ye multiply your petition, I will not listen to you; for your hands are full of blood.
[Isaiah] [1]:[16] Wash you, become clean; take away your wickednesses from your souls before mine eyes; cease from your wickednesses,
[Isaiah] [1]:[17] Learn to do well; seek out judgment, deliver one wronged, judge for the orphan, justify the widow.
[Isaiah] [1]:[18] And come, and let us be convicted, saith the Lord; and if your sins be as a scarlet thing, I will make them white as snow; and if they be as crimson, I will make them white as wool.
[Isaiah] [1]:[19] And if ye be willing, and hearken unto me, ye shall eat the good things of the land:
[Isaiah] [1]:[20] But if ye be not willing, neither hearken unto me, a sword shall devour you; for the mouth of the Lord hath spoken this.
[Isaiah] [1]:[21] How is the faithful city Zion become a harlot! (once) full of judgment, and in her righteousness did rest; but now murderers.
[Isaiah] [1]:[22] Your silver is base; thy hucksters mix the wine with water:
[Isaiah] [1]:[23] Thy rulers are disobedient; companions of thieves, that love gifts, that pursue a recompense; that judge not for orphans, and attend not to the cause of a widow.
[Isaiah] [1]:[24] Therefore thus saith the Ruler, the Lord of Hosts, Ah, the strong ones of Israel! for my wrath shall not cease upon mine adversaries, and I will exact judgment from mine enemies.
[Isaiah] [1]:[25] And I will turn mine hand upon thee, and burn thee into purity, and the disobedient will I destroy, and take away all transgressors from thee, and will humble all arrogant ones.
[Isaiah] [1]:[26] And I will set up thy judges as in former time, and thy counsellors as from the beginning; and after that thou shalt be called City of righteousness, faithful mother-city Zion.
[Isaiah] [1]:[27] For with judgment shall her captivity be saved, and with mercy.
[Isaiah] [1]:[28] And the transgressors and the sinners together shall be broken in pieces, and they that forsake the Lord shall be brought to an end.
[Isaiah] [1]:[29] For they shall be ashamed for their idols, which themselves desired, and shall be ashamed for their gardens, which they longed for.
[Isaiah] [1]:[30] For they shall be as a terebinth that hath cast off its leaves, and as a park with no water.
[Isaiah] [1]:[31] And their strength shall be as a stalk of flax, and their works as sparks of fire, and the transgressors and the sinners shall be burned up together, and there shall be none that shall quench them.
[Isaiah] [2]:[1] The word that came from the Lord to Isaiah the son of Amoz concerning Judah and concerning Jerusalem.
[Isaiah] [2]:[2] For in the last days shall the mountain of the Lord be clearly seen, and the house of God upon the top of the mountains, and it shall be uplifted above the hills, and all the nations shall come unto it.
[Isaiah] [2]:[3] And many nations shall go, and shall say, Come, let us go up into the mountain of the Lord, and into the house of the God of Jacob; and he will proclaim to us his way, and we will walk in it. For out of Zion shall a law come forth, and the word of the Lord from Jerusalem.
[Isaiah] [2]:[4] And he shall judge between the nations, and shall convince much people; and they shall beat their swords into ploughshares, and their spears into pruning hooks; and nation shall not any more take a sword against nation, and they shall not learn to war any more.
[Isaiah] [2]:[5] And now, O house of Jacob, come, let us walk by the light of the Lord.
[Isaiah] [2]:[6] For he hath given up his people, the house of Israel; because their country is filled with omens, as at the first, like the land of the Strangers, and many strange children are born to them.
[Isaiah] [2]:[7] For their country is filled with silver and gold, and there is no number to their treasures; and their land is filled with horses, and there is no number to their chariots:
[Isaiah] [2]:[8] And the land is filled with abominations, the works of their hands, and they worship things which their own fingers have made:
[Isaiah] [2]:[9] And a mean man boweth down, and a great man is humbled, and I will not let them be.
[Isaiah] [2]:[10] And now, enter into the rocks, and hide yourselves in the earth, from before the fear of the Lord, and from the glory of his strength, when he ariseth to shatter the earth.
[Isaiah] [2]:[11] For the eyes of the Lord (are) lifted high, but man (is) humble: and the uplifting of men shall be humbled, and the Lord alone shall be uplifted in that day.
[Isaiah] [2]:[12] For there is a day of the Lord of Hosts against every worker of pride, and overweening one, and against every one that is high and lofty, and they shall be humbled:
[Isaiah] [2]:[13] And against every cedar of Lebanon, of them that are high and lofty, and against every oak-tree of Bashan,
[Isaiah] [2]:[14] And against every mountain, and against every high hill,
[Isaiah] [2]:[15] And against every high tower, and against every high wall,
[Isaiah] [2]:[16] And against every ship of the sea, and against every sight of the beauty of ships:
[Isaiah] [2]:[17] And every man shall be humbled, and the uplifting of men shall fall, and the Lord alone shall be uplifted in that day.
[Isaiah] [2]:[18] And all the works of men's hands shall they hide away,
[Isaiah] [2]:[19] Carrying them into the caves, and into the rents of the rocks, and into the holes of the earth, from before the fear of the Lord, and from the glory of his strength, when he ariseth to shatter the earth.
[Isaiah] [2]:[20] In that day shall a man cast out his abominations, the silver and the golden, which he made to worship, to the vain ones and to the bats;
[Isaiah] [2]:[21] To go into the holes of the solid rock, and into the rents of the rocks, from before the face of the Lord, and from the glory of his strength, when he ariseth to shatter the earth.
[Isaiah] [3]:[1] Now behold, the ruler, the Lord of Hosts, will take away from Judah and from Jerusalem him that is strong and her that is strong, the strength of bread and the strength of water,
[Isaiah] [3]:[2] Giant, and strong man, and man of war, and judge, and prophet, and diviner, and elder,
[Isaiah] [3]:[3] And captain of fifty, and wonderful counsellor, and skilful artificer, and understanding listener,
[Isaiah] [3]:[4] And I will set up youths as their rulers, and mockers shall lord it over them.
[Isaiah] [3]:[5] And the people shall fall together, each against another, and each against his neighbour; the child shall stumble against the elder, the dishonoured against the honourable.
[Isaiah] [3]:[6] For aman shall take hold of his brother, or of his father's kinsman, saying, Thou hast a cloke, be thou our leader, and let my meat be subject to thee.
[Isaiah] [3]:[7] And he shall answer and say in that day, I will not be thy leader; for there is no bread in my house, neither a cloke; I will not be a leader of this people.
[Isaiah] [3]:[8] For Jerusalem is abandoned, and Judah is fallen down; and their tongues (are) with transgression, (they are) disobedient toward the Lord; wherefore now is their glory humbled.
[Isaiah] [3]:[9] And the shame of their face is risen up against them; and they have proclaimed their sin as of Sodom, and made it plain. Woe unto their soul! for they have counselled evil counsel against themselves,:
[Isaiah] [3]:[10] Saying, Let us bind the just, for he is of ill service to us: therefore shall they eat the fruit of their works.
[Isaiah] [3]:[11] Woe to the transgressor! evil shall befall him according to the works of his hands.
[Isaiah] [3]:[12] My people, your exactors glean you, and the tax gatherers shall lord it over you: my people, they that call you happy cause you to err, and confound the path of your feet.
[Isaiah] [3]:[13] But now shall the Lord stand up for judgment, and shall set up his people for judgment.
[Isaiah] [3]:[14] The Lord himself shall come to judgment with the elders of the people, and the rulers thereof. But ye, why did ye burn my vineyard, and the spoil of the poor is in your houses?
[Isaiah] [3]:[15] Why do ye wrong my people, and shame the face of the poor?
[Isaiah] [3]:[16] Thus saith the Lord, Because the daughters of Zion are uplifted, and walk with uplifted neck, and with winkings of the eyes, and in the passage of their feet both sweeping their skirts and mincing with their feet the while,
[Isaiah] [3]:[17] (So) also shall God humble the principal daughters of Zion, and the Lord shall discover their form
[Isaiah] [3]:[18] In that day; and the Lord will take away the glory of their apparel, and their adornments, and the braidings, and the fringes, and the crescents,
[Isaiah] [3]:[19] And the pendant, and the adornment of the face,
[Isaiah] [3]:[20] And the ordering of the adornment of their glory, and the bracelets, and the armlets, and the braiding (of hair), and the bangles, and the finger rings, and the earrings,
[Isaiah] [3]:[21] And the garments edged with purple, and shaded with purple,
[Isaiah] [3]:[22] And the hangings about the house, and the Laconian gauzes,
[Isaiah] [3]:[23] And the (garments) of fine linen, and of blue, and scarlet, and the fine linen embroidered with gold and with blue, and the reclining veils.
[Isaiah] [3]:[24] And there shall be, instead of a sweet smell, dust; and instead of a girdle thou shalt gird thyself with a rope, and instead of the golden adornment of the head thou shalt have baldness because of thy works, and instead of the purple shaded robe thou shalt gird thyself about with sackcloth.
[Isaiah] [3]:[25] And thy son, the fairest, whom thou lovest, shall fall by the sword, and your strong men shall fall by the sword, and shall be humbled:
[Isaiah] [3]:[26] And the coffers of your ornaments shall mourn, and thou shalt be left alone, and shalt be trodden into the earth.
[Isaiah] [4]:[1] And seven women shall take hold of one man, saying, We will eat our own bread, and wear our own clothing; only let thy name be called upon us; take away our reproach.
[Isaiah] [4]:[2] But in that day shall God shine in counsel with glory upon the earth, to uplift and to glorify that which is left of Israel.
[Isaiah] [4]:[3] And it shall be, that which is left behind in Zion, and left in Jerusalem, they shall be called holy, all they who were written unto life in Jerusalem.
[Isaiah] [4]:[4] For the Lord shall cleanse the filth of the sons and of the daughters of Zion, and shall purge the blood from the midst of them, by the spirit of judgment [[and the spirit of burning]].
[Isaiah] [4]:[5] And he shall come, and it shall be, every place of the mount Zion, and all that is round about her; a cloud shall shadow it by day, and like as when smoke and as a light of fire burneth by night; it shall be sheltered by all the glory (of the Lord),
[Isaiah] [4]:[6] And it shall be for a shadow from heat, and as a covering and a hiding place from stress and from rain storm.
[Isaiah] [5]:[1] Now will I sing for my beloved a song of my loved one for my vineyard. My beloved hath a vineyard on a hill-top, in a fertile place;
[Isaiah] [5]:[2] And I set a hedge around it, and fenced it, and planted a vine of Sorek, and built a tower in the midst of it, and digged a wine-fat therein; and I waited for it to bring forth a cluster of grapes, and it brought forth thorns.
[Isaiah] [5]:[3] And now, man of Judah, and ye inhabitants of Jerusalem, judge ye betwixt me and my vineyard.
[Isaiah] [5]:[4] What shall I do yet for my vineyard, that I have not done for it? because I waited for it to bring forth a cluster of grapes, but it brought forth thorns.
[Isaiah] [5]:[5] But now I will declare to you what I will do to my vineyard; I will take away the hedge thereof, and it shall be plundered; and destroy the wall thereof, and it shall be trodden down;
[Isaiah] [5]:[6] And I will abandon my vineyard, and it shall not be pruned, nor digged; and there shall come up into it thorns, as into a waste place; and I will command the clouds, to shower no rain upon it.
[Isaiah] [5]:[7] For the vineyard of the Lord of Hosts is the house of Israel, and the man of Judah a beloved young plant; I waited for (him) to bring forth judgment, but he brought forth transgression, and not righteousness, but a cry.
[Isaiah] [5]:[8] Woe, they that join house to house, and set field next to field, that they may rob somewhat from their neighbour; will ye dwell alone upon the earth?
[Isaiah] [5]:[9] For this was heard in the ears of the Lord of Hosts; For if houses become many, they shall be a desolation; great ones and fair, and there shall be none that inhabit them.
[Isaiah] [5]:[10] For where ten yoke of oxen plough, it shall yield one jar, and he that soweth six bushels shall get three measures.
[Isaiah] [5]:[11] Woe, they that rise up early, and follow strong drink; that tarry till late; for wine shall burn them up.
[Isaiah] [5]:[12] For with harp, and psaltery, and tabrets, and pipes, drink they their wine; but they regard not the works of the Lord, and the works of his hands they consider not.
[Isaiah] [5]:[13] Therefore is my people become captive, because they know not the Lord; and is become a multitude of dead bodies, because of famine and thirst for water.
[Isaiah] [5]:[14] And Hades enlarged his appetite, and opened his mouth, continually; and her glorious, and great, and rich ones, and her pestilences, shall descend.
[Isaiah] [5]:[15] And the mean man shall be humbled, and the great man shall be dishonoured, and the eyes that are lifted up shall be humbled:
[Isaiah] [5]:[16] And the Lord of Hosts shall be exalted in judgment, and God, the Holy One, shall be glorified in righteousness.
[Isaiah] [5]:[17] And they that are spoiled shall graze as bulls, and the waste places of them that are taken away shall lambs eat.
[Isaiah] [5]:[18] Woe, they that drag along their sins as with a long rope, and their transgressions as with a cow-hide chariot trace:
[Isaiah] [5]:[19] That say, Let him speedily bring near what he will do, that we may see; and let the counsel of the Holy One [[of Israel]] come, that we may perceive;
[Isaiah] [5]:[20] Woe, they that call evil good, and good evil; that set darkness as light, and light as darkness; that set bitter as sweet, and sweet as bitter!
[Isaiah] [5]:[21] Woe, they that are prudent in themselves, and have understanding in their own sight!
[Isaiah] [5]:[22] Woe, they that are strong among you, that drink wine, and the powerful ones, that mingle the strong drink:
[Isaiah] [5]:[23] That justify the impious for rewards' sake, and remove the justice of the just.
[Isaiah] [5]:[24] Therefore, like as a reed shall be burned by. coals of fire, and burned up by blazing flame, their root shall be as chaff, and their blossom shall go up as whirling dust: for they desired not the law of the Lord of Hosts, but they provoked the oracle of [[the Holy One of]] Israel.
[Isaiah] [5]:[25] And the Lord of Hosts was wroth with anger against his people, and laid his hand upon them, and smote them; and the mountains were provoked (to anger), and their carcases became as dung in the midst of the way. Amid all this his wrath is not turned away, but his hand is still upraised.
[Isaiah] [5]:[26] Therefore he shall raise up a signal among the nations afar off, and shall hiss unto them from the end of the earth; and behold, quickly they come swiftly.
[Isaiah] [5]:[27] They shall not [[hunger nor]] grow weary, nor nod, nor sleep, nor shall they loose their girdles from their loins, nor shall the thongs of their sandals be broken:
[Isaiah] [5]:[28] Whose darts are sharp, and their bows bent; their horses' hoofs are as solid rock; the wheels of their chariots were counted as a whirlwind.
[Isaiah] [5]:[29] They spring like lions, and are come up like a lion's whelp; and he shall take hold, and shall roar as a wild beast, and shall cast them out, and there shall be none that delivereth them,
[Isaiah] [5]:[30] And he shall roar because of them, in that day, as the voice of a surging sea; and they shall look upon the earth, and behold, cruel darkness in their dismay.
[Isaiah] [6]:[1] And it came to pass, in the year that king Uzziah died, I saw the Lord seated upon a throne, (that was) high and lifted up, and the house was full of his glory.
[Isaiah] [6]:[2] And seraphim stood around him; each one had six wings; and with twain they covered their face, and with twain they covered their feet, and with twain they did fly.
[Isaiah] [6]:[3] And they cried one to another and said, Holy, holy, holy, is the Lord of Hosts; all the earth is full of his glory.
[Isaiah] [6]:[4] And the lintel was lifted up, at the voice with which they cried, and the house was filled with smoke.
[Isaiah] [6]:[5] And I said, O wretched me, for I am amazed; for being (but) a man, and with impure lips, I dwell in the midst of a people with impure lips: and I have seen the King, the Lord of Hosts, with mine eyes.
[Isaiah] [6]:[6] And there was sent to me one of the seraphim, and in his hand he had a coal of fire, which he had taken with the tongs from the altar;
[Isaiah] [6]:[7] And he touched my mouth, and said, Lo, this hath touched thy lips, and shall take away thy transgressions, and shall purify thy sins.
[Isaiah] [6]:[8] And I heard the voice of the Lord, saying, Whom am I to send, and who will go to this people? And I said, Here am I; send me.
[Isaiah] [6]:[9] And he said, Go, and say to this people, By hearing ye shall hear, and not understand; and seeing ye shall see, and not perceive.
[Isaiah] [6]:[10] For the heart of this people is made fat, and with their ears they hear dully, and their eyes have they closed; lest at any time they should see with their eyes, and hear with their ears, and understand with their heart, and should return, when I will heal them.
[Isaiah] [6]:[11] And I said, Until when, Lord? And he said, Until cities be desolated, because they are not inhabited, and houses, because there are no men; and the land shall be left desolate.
[Isaiah] [6]:[12] And after this, God shall far remove men, and they that are left shall be multiplied upon the earth.
[Isaiah] [6]:[13] And still upon it is the tenth part, and again it shall be for plunder; as a terebinth, and as an oak, when it is torn from its place.
[Isaiah] [7]:[1] And it came to pass, in the days of Ahaz the son of Jotham, the son of Uzziah, king of Judah, there went up Rezin the king of Aram and Pekah the son of Remaliah, king of Israel, against Jerusalem to war against it, and they were not able to besiege it.
[Isaiah] [7]:[2] And it was reported to the house of David, saying, Aram hath made agreement with Ephraim; and his soul was distraught, and the soul of his people, like as when a tree in a forest is shaken with the wind.
[Isaiah] [7]:[3] And the Lord said unto Isaiah, Go forth to meet Ahaz, thou, and he that is left, Jashub thy son, at the pool of the upper road of the fuller's field;
[Isaiah] [7]:[4] And thou shalt say to him, Take heed, that thou be quiet; and fear thou not, neither let thy soul be faint, at these two logs of smoking firebrands; for when the anger of my wrath is come, I will heal again.
[Isaiah] [7]:[5] And (as for) the son of Aram and the son of Remaliah, because they have counselled evil counsel concerning thee, saying,
[Isaiah] [7]:[6] We will go up against Judah, and let us talk with them, and turn them away to us, and we will make king over them the son of ‘Tabeal:

[Isaiah] [7]:[11] Ask thee a sign from the Lord thy God, (down) to the depth or (up) to the height.
[Isaiah] [7]:[12] And Ahaz said, I will not ask, neither will I tempt the Lord.

[Isaiah] [7]:[17] But God shall bring upon thee, and upon thy people, and upon thy father's house, days that have not yet come since the day when he took away Ephraim from Judah; the king of the Assyrians.
[Isaiah] [7]:[18] And it shall be in that day, the Lord shall hiss to the flies, that which ruleth over a part of the river of Egypt, and to the bee which is in the land of the Assyrians.
[Isaiah] [7]:[19] And they shall all come, and shall rest in the valleys of the land, and in the holes of the rocks, and into the caverns, and into every cleft, and upon every tree.
[Isaiah] [7]:[20] In that day shall the Lord shave with the razor that is great and drunken, which is beyond the river of the king of the Assyrians; the head, and the hair of the feet, and the beard shall he take away.
[Isaiah] [7]:[21] And it shall be in that day, a man shall nourish a heifer of kine, and two sheep:
[Isaiah] [7]:[22] And it shall be, from their giving very much milk, butter and honey shall everyone eat, that is left upon the land.
[Isaiah] [7]:[23] And it shall be, in that day, every place, wheresoever there be a thousand vines at a thousand shekels, they shall be for barren land and thorn.
[Isaiah] [7]:[24] With arrow and bow shall they enter there; for barren and thorn shall all the land be;
[Isaiah] [7]:[25] And every mountain shall be deeply ploughed; and fear shall not come thither; for from the barren land and thorn it shall be for the feeding of a sheep, and the treading of an ox.

[Isaiah] [8]:[6] Because this people desire not the water of Siloam which goeth quietly, but desire to have Rezin and the son of Remaliah for king over you:
[Isaiah] [8]:[7] Therefore, behold, the Lord bringeth upon you the river's water, that is strong and mighty, the king of the Assyrians and his glory; and he shall go up upon every valley of yours, and walk about upon every wall of yours.

[Isaiah] [8]:[10] And whatsoever counsel ye take, the Lord shall scatter it: and whatsoever word ye speak, it shall not abide for you; for the Lord God is with us.
[Isaiah] [8]:[11] Thus saith the Lord God, With the strong hand do they dispute the passage of the way of this people, saying,
[Isaiah] [8]:[12] Never speak ye stubbornly; for all that this people speaketh is stubborn; but fear ye not the fear of it, nor be confounded:
[Isaiah] [8]:[13] Sanctify the Lord himself; and he himself shall be your Fear.
